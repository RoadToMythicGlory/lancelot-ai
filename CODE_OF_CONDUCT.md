# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Lancelot a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people who run broadcasts with this software

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments in any form
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sharing real customer data, schedules, credentials or personal details in issues or PRs

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, issues and PRs that are not aligned with this Code of
Conduct. Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private security advisory on this
repository or contacting the maintainer through the profile listed on GitHub.
